YouTube Music Party server

This address connects your player to your Telegram room. Music plays in your YouTube Music tab, not on this page.

In the extension popup, open Advanced / custom server and paste this URL into the server field. Only connect to a server you trust.

Use the room ID from your Telegram bot, then choose Join Room in the extension. Wait for Connected before controlling playback.
