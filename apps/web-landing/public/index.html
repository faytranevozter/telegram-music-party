You bring the tab.
The group brings the tunes.

Set up the player in desktop Chrome. Once it’s connected, group members can use Telegram on their phones or computers.

  1. Install the extension

    Add to Chrome from the Chrome Web Store. Chrome handles updates automatically.

  2. Register your group

    Add @xmsc_bot to your Telegram group and send /register to get your Room ID.

  3. Open your player

    Open YouTube Music, then open the extension popup from your browser toolbar.

  4. Join the room

    Paste the Room ID and choose Join Room. The default server is https://music.ytparty.my.id. Advanced / custom server is optional.

    Joining refreshes the music tab and may briefly interrupt playback.

  5. Wait for Connected

    Saved settings alone don’t mean you’re admitted. Once the popup shows Connected, search for a song with @xmsc_bot in Telegram, add it to the queue, and send /play.

Room busy? Leave on the existing player before choosing Retry join. Joining never replaces that player. Get help with joining, multiple tabs, or unconfirmed admission.
